What works well

Very clear separation of roles: the calendar chooses days, the meals window edits meals. That keeps the month view calm and easy to scan.

Main UX risk

If each day has several meals, the calendar can become too vague unless each date gives a small hint that something is planned.

Likely best behavior

Click a date, highlight it, show breakfast/lunch/dinner slots on the right, then choose meals from a reusable meal library below.
